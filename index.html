Hello, welcome to my page, my name is Rahim Sheikh, and I am a student at NOVA pursuing a degree in Cybersecurity.

This webpage contains information about mountains that I have researched and picked out of all of the mountains that exist around the world.

I chose mountains as my topic because they have always felt like some of the most peaceful and powerful places in the world. Many cultures see them as sacred, and people have told stories about them and treated them as special places for thousands of years. They are also home to animals like snow leopards, mountain goats, and eagles that have adapted to survive in rough, rocky terrain. Even the weather on a mountain can change within minutes, going from sunny skies to heavy storms. That mix of beauty and unpredictability is what makes them so interesting to me.

Making this page was a great way to practice the skills I am learning in my classes. As a cybersecurity student, I enjoy research and paying attention to detail, and both were important while putting this project together. I also practiced HTML and CSS by organizing the content with headings, lists, colors, and a custom banner. Building it taught me how much planning goes into even a simple webpage. I hope everyone who visits learns something new and enjoys this page as much as I enjoyed making it.

Mountain Facts

  • Mountains are formed mainly by tectonic plates colliding or pushing together
  • The Himalayas are still rising a few millimeters each year.
  • Mountains cover roughly one quarter of Earth's land surface.
  • Many of the world's major rivers begin in mountain snow and glaciers.
  • Mountains air gets thinner as you climb, so less oxygen is available
